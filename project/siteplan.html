Website Planning Document

WDD 131 Final Project

Site Name

AI Tools Hub

I chose the name AI Tools Hub because it clearly represents a website that introduces and explains useful Artificial Intelligence tools in one place. The word "Hub" suggests a central location where visitors can learn, compare, and discover AI tools for education, creativity, coding, productivity, and everyday use.

Optional Domain: aitoolshub.com

Site Purpose

AI Tools Hub is an educational website designed to introduce visitors to popular Artificial Intelligence tools. The website explains what each tool does, compares their features, highlights their strengths and weaknesses, and helps users choose the right AI tool for their personal, academic, or professional needs.

Scenarios

  • Which AI tool is best for writing assignments and research?
  • What is the difference between ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude?
  • Which AI tools are free to use?
